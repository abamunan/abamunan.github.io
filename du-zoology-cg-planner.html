How CGPA is calculated

  1. For every course: total marks = attendance + in-course + final exam. Any fractional total is rounded up to the next whole mark.
  2. Convert the total to a percentage of the course marks (1 credit = 25 marks), then to a letter grade and grade point using the scale below.
  3. GPA (one year) = sum of (grade point × credits) ÷ sum of credits attempted.
  4. CGPA (2nd, 3rd and 4th year) = total grade points earned up to date ÷ total credits attempted. The 1st year result is a GPA, and it carries into every later CGPA.
GPA = Σ (Grade point × Credits) ÷ Σ Credits attempted

Example: Zool 101 is 75 marks. A total of 60 marks is 80%, which is A+ (4.00). In this calculator the first-year CGPA shown above is the 1st-year GPA over the 28 credits listed in the table.

Other important details

Exams and in-course tests

  • At least 2 in-course tests for a 4-credit course and at least 1 for a 2-credit course. Missing a test counts as zero.
  • Final exam length: 3 hours (4 credits), 2.5 hours (3 credits), 2 hours (2 credits).
  • Final exam scripts are marked by two examiners and the marks are averaged. A third examiner is used if the two differ by more than 20%.
  • Minimum 75% attendance to sit the final exam without penalty. 60% to under 75% requires non-collegiate fees. Under 60% is not allowed to sit.

Earned credits

  • D or higher counts as earned credits. An F does not count.
  • Total for the degree is 140 credits: 28 + 32 + 38 + 42 across the four years.

Promotion

  • 1st to 2nd year: GPA of at least 2.00 and all courses passed. Up to 8 failed credits with GPA 2.00 or above means promotion on probation.
  • 2nd to 3rd year: CGPA of at least 2.25. Probation is possible with CGPA from 2.00 to under 2.25 and no F, or with up to 8 failed credits.
  • 3rd to 4th year: CGPA of at least 2.50. Probation is possible with CGPA from 2.25 to under 2.50 and no F, or with up to 8 failed credits.

Retake, improvement and graduation

  • Retake: final exam only, up to 8 credits, 4 to 6 weeks after results, and the transcript is marked.
  • Improvement: once, with the next batch, up to 8 credits per year. The improved grade point is used.
  • Graduation needs 140 credits, no F grade, and a CGPA of at least 2.50. Maximum time is six academic years.
  • Dean's Award: CGPA of 3.75 or above after completing the four years.
